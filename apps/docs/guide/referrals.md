# Referrals

Every account has a referral link. When someone you invite installs the extension and their node completes its first verified deliveries, you both benefit.

## How it works

- **You** earn a **10% bonus** on your referral's contributor earnings for their first 12 months. The bonus is paid by Root Network from its 30% share; it is never deducted from your referral's earnings.
- **Your referral** receives a **$2.00 welcome credit** once their node has delivered its first 1 GB.

There are no secondary or tertiary tiers. We would rather pay a clean bonus on people you actually know than build a pyramid.

## Finding your link

Your link is on the **Referrals** card of your dashboard. It looks like `https://earn.rootnetwork.co/?ref=YOURCODE`.

## Rules

- One account per person. Referring yourself, or accounts you control, forfeits referral bonuses on both sides.
- Referrals must be real people on real residential connections. Automated sign-ups are detected and removed.
- Bonuses are paid at settlement, alongside your regular earnings, and appear as a separate line in your history.
