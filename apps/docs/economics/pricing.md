# Pricing for AI labs

Labs pay per gigabyte delivered and verified. Nothing else is billed: failed deliveries, rejected requests and policy blocks are free.

## Rates

| Plan | Rate per GB | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Research** | $1.50 | Academic and non-profit labs, up to 2 TB / month |
| **Standard** | $1.25 | Production data pipelines, no volume minimum |
| **Volume** | From $0.95 | 50 TB / month and above, annual agreement |

Rates are for the default region pool (North America and Western Europe). Other regions are priced by demand and shown in the API's rate card endpoint.

## What is included

- Region targeting at country level
- Concurrency up to your plan's limit
- Verified delivery receipts and settlement commitments
- Dashboard with per-request logs, spend controls and alerts
- Support with a 24-hour response target

## Billing

- **Prepaid** balances for new labs; usage is deducted at each settlement window.
- **Monthly invoicing** for labs with an established history, net 30.
- Payment by wire or USDC.

## Spend controls

Every request carries a spend cap. Labs also set a monthly ceiling and optional per-project budgets in the dashboard. Traffic stops when a ceiling is reached; it does not overrun.

## Getting access

Labs are onboarded manually. See [Access & API](/labs/access).
